## Q1: What document formats are supported by Team Knowledge Finder?
**Answer**: The platform supports PDF (`.pdf`), Microsoft Word (`.docx`), Markdown (`.md`), Plain Text (`.txt`), CSV (`.csv`), Excel (`.xlsx`), and PowerPoint (`.pptx`).

## Q2: What is the maximum upload file size?
**Answer**: The maximum upload size is 25 megabytes per document.

## Q3: How is document security handled?
**Answer**: Uploaded documents are processed server-side with RBAC access levels (`public`, `team`, `developer`, `admin`) and indexed in encrypted storage.
